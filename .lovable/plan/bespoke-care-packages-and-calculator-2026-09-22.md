# Bespoke care packages and calculator

## What will change
- Reframe the three offers as flexible care levels rather than fixed property types: **Essential Care**, **Home Ready**, and **VIP Care**.
- Make garden and pool care optional across every level, so each plan suits anything from a flat or townhouse with a yard to a finca or villa with a pool.
- Keep the free Property Vault prominent on every plan and make the included monthly maintenance hours the clearest difference between levels.
- Position VIP Care as the full property-health service, including proactive checks of air conditioning, plumbing, electrics, fixtures and general condition.

## Bespoke plan journey
1. Welcome: explain that the one-minute journey creates a plan around the individual home.
2. Property type: choose **Flat**, **Townhouse**, **Villa**, or **Finca** using simple architectural line illustrations.
3. Outdoor space: choose garden or yard size, including a **No garden / yard** option.
4. Pool: choose **No pool** or a pool size.
5. Care level: compare the three levels and their sample included maintenance hours.
6. Extra services: choose optional service hours and the likely type of work.
7. Plan length and quote: move commitment to the final step, showing monthly try-out or **3 months — 5% off**, **6 months — 8% off**, and **12 months — 10% off**, then display the tailored monthly total and enquiry button.

## Sample included hours
- **Essential Care:** routine scheduled visits; no general maintenance hours included.
- **Home Ready:** 2 general maintenance hours per month included.
- **VIP Care:** 4 general maintenance hours per month included, alongside proactive whole-property checks.

## Pricing approach
- Keep the current prices as starting points for now, then adjust the indicative total according to property type, outdoor-space size, pool size, care level, extra hours and term discount.
- Clearly label all figures as indicative until the team has viewed the property.

## Technical details
- Update the homepage cards and the existing plan calculator without adding data storage or changing the enquiry delivery method.
- Use lightweight inline line-art property illustrations that inherit the existing green/coral design tokens.
- Preserve the established rounded cards, cream surfaces, solid coral buttons and mobile-friendly layout.
- Update the “How we work” wording so it matches the new bespoke journey and discount structure.
- Verify the complete flow on desktop and mobile, including the no-garden and no-pool paths.
